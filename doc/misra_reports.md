## Executive summary

The `mc_comm` and `mc_msg` production sources were analyzed after remediation.
The module scope has no open Mandatory or Required MISRA C:2012 findings. The
changes removed every reported instance of parameter modification (Rule 17.8),
pointer arithmetic (Rule 18.4), and `#undef` usage (Rule 20.5) from the scoped
modules. Open Advisory findings remain visible below and are not represented as
compliant or suppressed.
